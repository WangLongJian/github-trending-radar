- **分类领域**: `🤖 AI & Agent Skills` | **语言**: `JavaScript` | ⭐ **365** (~365 (7天内新建))
- **官方介绍**: Video as code: canvas scenes rendered with WebCodecs in headless Chrome, a review viewer, and agent skills that build the video from your references.

### 🇬🇧 [Alpha John 专版 · English]
```text
AI video generators make glossy, useless slop. Traditional editors cost $3k/month and take 5 business days.

Meet motlab: Video as code.

Every scene is just a Canvas function `renderAt(t)` rendered frame-by-frame via WebCodecs in headless Chrome. 

You feed your AI agent a folder of assets (logos, UI footage, fonts, audio) + a prompt. The agent writes the code, previews it in a frame-by-frame web viewer, takes your feedback, and exports crisp 4K MP4.

Why this is a game changer for solo builders:
✅ Deterministic & pixel-perfect (no hallucinated gibberish)
✅ Programmatic programmatic iterations in seconds
✅ Native web tech (Canvas API + WebCodecs)
✅ Zero AI video models involved

This is what real leverage looks like in 2025.

🔗 https://github.com/staryone/motlab
```
